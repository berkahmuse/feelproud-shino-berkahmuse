## The scene

> Dad finally taught me to ride a bike today — I only fell twice.
> Both times he fell first, on purpose, so I wouldn't feel alone on the ground.

A small domestic moment: a dad teaching his kid to ride a bike, falling on purpose so the kid never feels alone on the ground. Warm, a little funny, and heartfelt — the kind of line that makes a proud judge smile.
